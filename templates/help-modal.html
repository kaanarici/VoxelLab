About this build
Version

VoxelLab is an experimental, local-first research imaging workbench built end-to-end through human-directed AI. It keeps source files local when possible and makes calibration and provenance boundaries explicit. Not for clinical use.

Click a finding in the sidebar to jump to that slice. Click a measurement to delete it. The floating AI card shows the observation for the current slice. Not a diagnosis.
